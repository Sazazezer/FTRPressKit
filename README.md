## Description

### Short Description

Feeding The Velociraptors is a retro-inspired horror comedy adventure where you take on the role of a theme park employee trying to cover up a very serious dinosaur-related problem. Explore the park, gather evidence, make questionable decisions, and avoid becoming lunch as you work towards one of several possible endings.

### Long Description

Feeding The Velociraptors is a retro-inspired horror comedy adventure set in a dinosaur theme park where things have gone very, very wrong. You play as Paul, a definitely ordinary employee who finds himself caught up in a situation involving escaped velociraptors, eaten CEOs, and an increasing number of problems that need to be dealt with before anyone starts asking too many questions.

Exploration and investigation are at the heart of the experience. As you move through the park, you'll search your surroundings, uncover evidence, interact with survivors, and find a way to escape your fate. The information you discover can be used to influence how events unfold, giving you choices about how you deal with the increasingly chaotic situation.

## Features

### Exploration

The game hosts forty separate locations for players to investigate and gain entry to, from the confines of the Administrative building, to the dense jungle foilage and even a car park! Players can search these areas in details, with many interactive parts that will either aid in their escape, cause more trouble, or just have stupid things happen.

### Mechanics

With a detailed traversal system, players can investigate the world of Cretaceous Corners (a legally distinct Dinosaur park) similar to the exploration mechanics of PS1-era horror games, but in a first person, point-and-click format.

Throughout the game are various puzzles that players need to solve to progress. The puzzles require a lot of out of the box thinking, with sometimes absrud solutions.

## The Story

You are Paul! Working as a low level administrator for the finest theme park to ever host realistic dinosaurs, you have found yourself in quite the pickle. Due to a probable accident on your part, the velociraptors are now loose in the park. They’ve eaten the CEO, the head of security, the head of research, the plucky child hacker and the local game hunter specialist, and now they thirst for even more human flesh.

They’re a lot less scarier than what we know of raptors from those movies, but the little ankle biters can still shred your ankles to pieces using their biting ability.

The only thing keeping you alive is the Administrative building’s lock-down system. Unfortunately, due to other factors that were probably an accident on your part, that system is running out of power. There's little food, no communications and little time left before the raptors get in.

Your only hope is the helicopter on the other side of the perfectly safe scenic woods. If the five of you can get to it, you’ll be good, but there’s no way the raptors won’t notice you.

The only option left is a sacrifice. One of you must distract the raptors long enough for the others to get to the helicopter. Lucky for your lack of debating skills, everyone else already talked it over when you were on break, and they’ve decided on your behalf that you should be the one to make this honorable sacrifice.

You have an hour before your noble sacrifice. Take this time to wander the building, reflect on your life, and think about how you got into this situation.

**Do not attempt to escape.**
